Before Storytelling · Bingo Warm-Up

1 card per team · 4×4
🎫 Give each team a card and let them write numbers 1 to 16 inside (one minute!).
❓ Ask the questions below. Correct answer → call a number between 1 and 16. Keep going until a team completes a line — bingo! 🏆

Tell the Story · pp. 4–11

Tr 1 · Finish it together
Don't finish your sentences — stop and let the class complete the line for you! 🗣️
Tr 2 · Repeat with tone + gesture
After you, students repeat each line with proper tone and do a gesture. 👍
