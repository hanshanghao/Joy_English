🔤 Teacher's Note — The -s / -es Rule READ ME FIRST

Unit 13 is the first time children must add an S to the verb. Almost every spoken sentence here depends on it: want → wants, eat → eats, drink → drinks. Children in Taiwan often drop the S or add it everywhere — this note explains the rule before the lesson starts.

S Who gets the S?

Only he, she, it and one person by name get the extra s. Everybody else uses the plain verb.

I / You / We / They → want He / She / It → wants

🎯 The full table

He / She / Itwantseatsdrinks

❗ DOES already has the S

In a question we say does + want — not “does wants”. The S has already moved from the verb to does!

What DOES she WANT? ✓ What DOES she WANTS? ✗

⚠️ Mistake 1 — no S at all

“She want bread.” Fix: point at a girl and hold up an imaginary S, then drop it on the end of want.

⚠️ Mistake 2 — S on everybody

“I wants bread.” Fix: only one person is being talked about. Say “I” and the S disappears.

⚠️ Mistake 3 — wants in questions

“What does she wants…?” Fix: the S lives on DOES now, so the verb goes back to plain want.
