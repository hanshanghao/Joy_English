🔤 Teacher's Note — The R Phonics READ ME FIRST

Unit 13 is full of the letter R: breakfast, for, dinner, rice, orange, Peter. British English is NON-rhotic — the R is only sounded before a vowel. Taiwanese children often say every R or swap R for L. Read this note in a British accent before the lesson starts.

R How to make it (British)

Bend your tongue up and back so the tip sits just behind your top teeth — but it does NOT touch. Lips pull forward a little. Hold it and say “rrr…”. Never roll it like a motorbike.

rrr… rice · red · rabbit

🇬🇧 British = NON-rhotic

The R is only sounded before a vowel. Before a consonant, or at the end of a word → no R sound.

breakfast = “brek-fuhst” for = “faw”

✅ R + a vowel — SAY the R

When a vowel follows, the British R is clear: rice, orange, read, red, rabbit, rain.

rice ✓ orange ✓

🔇 In this unit the R is often silent

breakfast · for · dinner · Peter · are — the R is at the end or before a consonant.

for breakfast — no R dinner — no R

⚠️ Mistake 1 — rhotic “brek-fást”

“brek-fást”, “faw-r”. Fix: drop the R before a consonant: breakfast, for, dinner.

⚠️ Mistake 2 — L for R

“lice” for rice. Fix: /l/ = tongue tip DOWN on the teeth ridge. /r/ = tongue tip UP, touching nothing.
