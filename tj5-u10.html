Teacher Guide · How to run the 50 minutes
Timing0–10 Warm-up · 10–20 Sight Words · 20–30 Values · 30–40 Lucky Die · 40–50 Final Review. Keep a brisk pace — the timer in the progress bar helps.
Classroom managementStudents stay seated until called. One student writes or chooses one word. Teams correct their own mistakes before asking the teacher.
Correction techniqueNever say "wrong" — say "listen again" and repeat the model. Let the team help first, then give the answer.
AudioEvery speaker is the browser's British-English voice (en-GB). Choose Slow / Normal / Fast in the top bar. If no British voice is installed the page says so and uses the closest English voice.

Teacher Guide · Writing Relay
Timing3–10 min
ObjectiveWrite and say a full sentence with is/are and in front of/behind.
PreparationTwo team areas on the board; this page on screen.
ManagementStudents stay seated until called. Each student writes or selects ONLY one word, then passes on. Read the finished sentence together.
CorrectionIf a word is wrong, the team fixes it before the next student. Teacher confirms only after the team's attempt.
Teacher: "Team A, get ready. Team B, get ready. One student, one word!" Teacher: "Read the whole sentence together, please."
Transition"Excellent writing! Now — new words. Look at the pictures."

Sentences

Teacher Guide · Lucky Die
Timing30–40 min
ObjectiveReview is/are, next, How, Where and in front from Units 6–9.
RulesShow one sentence. A student rolls. If the word fits the gap, the team wins a point and everyone reads the sentence.
Fair retryIf a word doesn't fit, no penalty — keep rolling or use "Next sentence". Turn on Practice mode to go straight through every sentence.
ManagementAlternate teams. The reader must say the whole sentence, not just the word.
