Patterns

SINGULAR & PLURAL
Rule: one grape = many grapes. We add s or es to make a plural. The underlined words are the key words in the pattern. Click any sentence to hear it!
Main pattern
Grapes grow on vines.
One grape grows on one vine.
Grape + s = Grapes  ·  vine + s = vines
Coconuts grow on palm trees.
One coconut grows on one palm tree.
coconut + s = Coconuts  ·  tree + s = trees
Bananas grow on banana plants.
One banana grows on one banana plant.
banana + s = Bananas  ·  plant + s = plants
Cherries grow on trees.
One cherry grows on one tree.
cherry → cherr + ies = Cherries (y → i + es)
Watermelons grow on vines.
One watermelon grows on one vine.
watermelon + s = Watermelons
Tomatoes grow on tomato plants.
One tomato grows on one tomato plant.
tomato + es = Tomatoes (o → + es)
Honeydews grow on vines.
One honeydew grows on one vine.
honeydew + s = Honeydews
I like guavas.
I like one guava, too!
guava + s = Guavas
Plural rules in this unit: most fruits just add s (grape → grapes) · words ending in o add es (tomato → tomatoes) · words ending in y change to ies (cherry → cherries).
