Teaching Objectives

U17
  • Understand and read aloud the dialogue about chores.
  • Listen to, recite, and read the dialogue fluently.
  • Learn the vocabulary: sweep the floor · wipe the table · walk the dog · read the newspaper · water the flowers.
  • Ask and answer yes/no questions about frequency with do / don't + usually.
  • Use the frequency adverbs: always · usually · often · sometimes · seldom · never.
  • Preview the third-person -s verb forms: sweeps · wipes · walks · reads · waters.
🗣️ Magic Words: “To the top!” (超越巅峰!) and “Keep it up!” (继续努力!) — cheer your teams on!
🔍

Difficulties & Reminders

Watch out!
  • Some students forget the ending -s in “waters the flowers” and the word the in “read the newspaper”.
  • Some students misplace usually in a negative answer: ❌ No, I usually don't. → ✅ No, I don't usually sweep the floor in the morning.
  • We say in the morning / in the afternoon / in the evening, but at night. Don't forget!
  • Each student gets only one shot with the sticky ball per round — more English, less throwing!

Patterns · Frequency Questions

15 minutes
Do you / they usually read the newspaper at night?
❓ Do you usually read the newspaper at night?
✅ Yes, I usually read the newspaper at night. / Yes, I do.
❌ No, I don't usually read the newspaper at night. / No, I don't.
🐕 They usually walk the dog on Sundays.

Substitution Machine

Build & Speak
✅ The frequency sandwich: the adverb sits after “usually don't” / before the verb — “No, I don't usually sweep…”. The adverb never changes in the answer.

The -s Rule

he · she
With he / she, the verb takes an -s: sweep → sweeps, wipe → wipes, walk → walks, read → reads, water → waters. Circle the -s and the word the in red so students notice them!
Does he usually walk the dog in the morning?
🐕 Does Peter usually walk the dog in the morning? — Yes, he usually walks the dog in the morning.
