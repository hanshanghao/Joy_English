Word Flashcards 21–30

tap to sound out & spell
This is the word side. Watch for the red circles ⭕ — they show something special!
1️⃣ 21 to 29: the red circle hugs the little dash ( – ) — there is always a hyphen between “twenty” and the ones word.
2️⃣ thirty: the red circle hugs “ty”! tw‑en‑ty … thir‑ty — both end in ty! 🎉
🧑‍🏫 Teacher: Click a flashcard to sound out and spell its word. After all ten words, press “Place flashcards under the numbers”.
