🃏 Word Flashcards

This is the word side. Watch for the red circles ⭕ — they show something special!

🔎 The mystery of the red circles!
1️⃣ For 21 to 29, the red circle hugs the little dash ( – ). When we write these numbers there is always a hyphen between “twenty” and the ones word.
2️⃣ Look at thirty — the red circle hugs “ty”! Tw-en-ty … thir-ty — both end in “ty”! 🎉
🧑‍🏫 Teacher: Click a flashcard to sound out and spell its word. After all ten words, press “Place flashcards under the numbers”.
