🔤 Teacher's Note — The R Phonics in Unit 6 RREAD ME FIRST

Almost every target word in this lesson carries the letter R. Children in Taiwan usually need two things: the British way to make /r/, and a reminder to keep the tongue back for /r/ but down for /l/. Read this note before the lesson.

R How to make it (British)

Bend your tongue up and back so the tip sits just behind your top teeth — but it does NOT touch. Lips pull forward a little. Hold it and say “rrr…”. Never roll it like a motorbike.

rrr… room full sentence

🇬🇧 British = NON-rhotic

In British English the R is only sounded before a vowel. In this unit the R is always before a vowel, so you always say it: bedroom, bathroom, living room, dining room, where, are, there.

car = “cah”

⚠️ Mistake 1 — L for R

“wiving room”, “bedloom”, “the coww”. Fix: /l/ = tongue tip DOWN on the teeth ridge. /r/ = tongue tip UP, touching nothing.

⚠️ Mistake 2 — Extra vowel

“bedrooom”, “rooom”, “breeeathroom”. Fix: the R is a small quiet sound, not a long vowel. Say the vowel once, then close your lips fast.

⚠️ Mistake 3 — Rolling the R

Rerolling the tongue (American style). Fix: the British /r/ never touches the roof of the mouth. If you can feel a bump, it is too far back.

✅ Say these R words

room · bedroom · bathroom · dining room · living room · where · are · there · They're · Where's the cow?

bedroom bathroom dining room living room

The R Sound — British /r/

PHONICS
R
Click the big R to hear the pure British sound.
1️⃣ Tongue UP and BACK, tip just behind the top teeth.
2️⃣ It does NOT touch anything.
3️⃣ Lips pull forward slightly, like a small smile.
4️⃣ No rolling. The British /r/ is quiet and quick.

in the + room, but at home

KEY CONTRAST
💡 in goes with a room · at goes with home. Click a sentence: ✅ = correct, ❌ = wrong. The wrong one is fixed and spoken for you.
She is in the kitchen.in the kitchen ✅
She is at home.at home ✅
He is in the bedroom.in the bedroom ✅
She is in the home.in the home ❌
He is at the kitchen.at the kitchen ❌
He is in home.in home ❌
🧠 Memory hook for children: “In” = inside a room (like inside a box). “At home” = at the whole house. We do not say “at the kitchen”.
