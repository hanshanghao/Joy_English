👩‍🏫 Teacher's How to Play ⏱ 10 MIN
  1. Voices: tap 🔊 on any card — the class listens and repeats 3 times, faster each round. ♀ = British lady, ♂ = British gentleman.
  2. Hello Chants: tap each card to hear it, then chorus together. ▶ Play the whole chant runs them in order — ⏹ Stop pauses it.
  3. Slap the Board: show the word. The first team to slap the screen and shout the word wins a point for their team!
  4. Warm-up Race: the team in turn picks an answer. Right = points · Wrong = 0, and the turn passes on by itself.
  5. Keys: press Space to swap turns · use +1 +2 +5 −1 ✎ on the team cards to fix scores by hand.
🏆 Scoring: every correct answer = +1 point (change to +2 / +3 at the top). Wrong answers never take points away. The sticky scoreboard always shows both teams.

The Four Review Patterns

1️⃣ Unit 11 · Favourite subject & class times What is …? / When …?

What is your favourite subject?
My favourite subject is art.
When do you have art class?
I have art class every Friday.
When does she have science class?
She has science class every Tuesday.
✏️ British note: the book prints favorite — we write favourite. After she / he / it it is has (never “haves”).

2️⃣ Unit 12 · Do / Does questions Yes, I do. / No, he does not.

Do you surf the Net on Mondays?
Yes, I do.
Does he go to the library on Fridays?
No, he does not.
He goes to the library at the weekend.
She studies English and watches a film.
✏️ do / does + base verb: Do you surf? Does he go? — no -s! But: He surfs. He goes. She studies. She watches.
📖 Unit 12 pattern page
📖 study → studies · surf → surfs · watch → watches

3️⃣ Unit 13 · What do you want …? want / drink

What do you want for lunch?
I want steak for lunch.
What does she drink for breakfast?
She drinks soybean milk for breakfast.
✏️ What does she DRINK? — no -s after does. But She DRINKS … — yes -s after she. The same with want / wants, eat / eats.
📖 Unit 13 pattern page
📖 want → wants · drink → drinks

4️⃣ Unit 14 · Do you …? / Do they …? / Does he …? food questions

Do you want dumplings for dinner?
Do they drink milk tea for breakfast?
Does he eat beef noodles for lunch?
Yes, he does. He eats beef noodles for lunch.
✏️ Answer short first: Yes, I do. / No, they do not. Then add a full sentence: They drink milk tea for breakfast.

🇬🇧

Six Culture Cards

TAP TO HEAR
💡 Tap ♀ / ♂ to hear the card in a British accent. Show the class that British English has two friendly speakers too.

🧮 Maths or math?

British children say maths — the book prints math. Both mean the same subject!

🎬 Film or movie?

Britain: a film at the cinema. America: a movie at a movie theater.

⚽ Football or soccer?

In Britain it is football — and we love it! Americans call the same game soccer.

📖 Favourite or favorite?

Britain spells it favourite. The book prints favorite. In this class we write favourite.

🎩 Please and thank you

British people say please, thank you, sorry and excuse me a lot. Try it today!

☕ Afternoon tea

At about four o'clock many British families have a cup of tea and some biscuits (Americans say cookies).
