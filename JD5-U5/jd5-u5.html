PERIOD 1 What Are They Wearing? · 40 min
🎥

Presentation 1 · Q&A + Video

10 min · p.57
Steps
1. Look out of the window and describe the weather: “It is sunny.” Then ask: “When it's sunny, do you wear a T-shirt?” Show the flashcard. Let 2–3 students say why they do or don't wear T-shirts.
2. Say: “We wear different clothes depending on the weather. Let's watch the video and see how Dr. Smarty wears for different types of weather in a fashion show.”
3. Tap Presentation ▶ to play the video. Pause and ask:
a) At 0:23 — a fashion show is when people wear nice clothes and walk in front of everyone.
b) At 1:06 — What will you wear when it's hot?
4. After the video, ask what students would wear when it is hot, warm, cold or cool.
💡 Notes: If students can't answer, review Tales of Joy 3: It is warm / hot / cold / cool. The p.26 video shows Dr. Smarty modelling all four seasons on the runway. Open books to pp.26–27 and point: “You will wear a blouse and sandals.”

Presentation 2 · Picture Telling + Q&A + Look and Say

22 min · p.58
Steps
1. Tap Presentation ▶ and pause on the first slide. Tell the class that Justin and Joyce are also at the fashion show — ask students to look for them, then one student circles them with a marker.
2. Continue the slides to:
a) introduce the clothing items,
b) get students to practise saying the target sentence.
3. Ask the question on the slide — the class answers with the pattern “She is wearing a red dress.” Divide the class into teams, show the next questions and let each team discuss, then choose a team to answer.
4. Students open their books to pp.26–27 and read the dialogue sentences aloud.
💡 Note: The presentation slides are interactive — make sure you get the students to share their thoughts.

E-teaching · Listen and Choose

8 min · p.59
Steps
1. Tap Activity ⧉ and choose Act. “Listen and Choose.”
2. Do the first question with the class. Ask what each girl is wearing, tap to play the recording, and have the class tell you the correct answer.
3. Get a student to come to the front and tap on a picture to show the results.
4. Divide the class into three teams and number each student.
5. Switch to three-player mode.
6. Go to the next questions. Give each team time to talk about what he or she is wearing, then call a number — those students come out and tap their answer.
7. Tap ✅ to check the answers.
💡 How to play: Students listen to the recording and look at three pictures, then tap the picture that best matches the recording. Note: Students may forget the English names of clothing items, so a group discussion before calling on a student helps.
PERIOD 1 + 2 What's Wrong? · 20 min (10 + 10)
❓

What's Wrong? · Steps 1–5

10 min · p.60–61
Goal: students choose appropriate clothing for different occasions · Pattern: “He should wear sneakers.”
1. Tap Presentation ▶ and pause on the first slide. Roll the digital die and ask the class to say what they are wearing for the number rolled.
2. Divide the class into teams and roll the die. Each team discusses the answer, then answers with the pattern “She is wearing a T-shirt and a dress.”
3. Continue the slides to:
a) think and discuss how to dress properly for different occasions,
b) guide students to finish the textbook activity — Stick.
4. On the Comfort / Respect slide, simplify the language: we wear sandals to the beach because they feel comfortable — but at school it is not polite to wear sandals; we should wear shoes. (Students do not need to say the two words.)
5. Number the students. Each team discusses first, then call a number — those students stand up, and the first to stand tells the answer. Award a point to that student's team!

PERIOD 2 Dress Them Up! · 35 min + Communication Book · 5 min
🎨

Dress Them Up! · Steps 1–5

35 min · p.29 / p.64–65
Goal: students dress paper dolls using stickers and describe what they are wearing
1. Tap Presentation ▶ and use the slides to guide students through the “Dress Them Up!” textbook activity — “Oh no, these dolls need something to wear! Let's dress them up.”
2. Give examples of where stickers go: “Can you put your hat on your foot?” or “Can you wear your dress upside down?”
3. Continue through the slides. Give students a few minutes to think, choose, peel off the stickers and place them on the boy and the girl — e.g. “The boy will go to school. It is cold and rainy. What should he wear?”
4. While the students are dressing up the dolls, walk around and check how they are doing.
5. Show the page and demonstrate how to introduce the two dolls, then ask students to introduce their dolls to the student next to them: “This is my doll. Her name is Anna. It's hot and sunny. She is wearing a dress and a cap.”
💡 Note: If time is not allowed, ask students to share one doll with the student next to them.

Class Activity 2 · Fashion Stylist

15 min · p.66
Props: Unit 5 worksheet (find the slide with the Open file icon and print one per student), scissors and glue
1. Tap Activity ⧉ and choose Act. “Fashion Stylist.”
2. Follow the instructions on the slides. Give students enough time to think, choose and cut out the clothing items they need and put the items together on Part A.
1. Who are these clothing items for — a man, a woman, a boy, or a girl?
2. Where can people wear them to? A party? Dinner? The beach?
3. On the last two slides, demonstrate how to introduce your design, then ask students to share their design with the student next to them.
💡 Note: Walk around and check how the students are doing. Assist them if needed.
