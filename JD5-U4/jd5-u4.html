🔤 Teacher's Note — The R Phonics in Unit 4 RREAD ME FIRST

The star R words of this unit are roll · run · rabbit · reach · ready. Children in Taiwan usually need two things: the British way to make /r/, and a reminder that British English is non-rhotic — the R is only sounded before a vowel. Read this note before the lesson.

R How to make it (British)

Bend your tongue up and back so the tip sits just behind your top teeth — but it does NOT touch. Lips pull forward a little. Hold it and say “rrr…”. Never roll it like a motorbike.

rrr… roll full sentence

🇬🇧 British = NON-rhotic

The R is only sounded before a vowel. In roll, run, rabbit, reach, ready the R is always before a vowel, so you always say it. But in after and before the R is silent: “ah-fuh”, “bee-foh”.

after = ah-fuh before = bee-foh

⚠️ Mistake 1 — L for R

“woll!”, “wun!”, “wabbit!”. Fix: /l/ = tongue tip DOWN on the teeth ridge. /r/ = tongue tip UP, touching nothing.

⚠️ Mistake 2 — Rolling the R

Rolling the tongue like a motorbike. Fix: the British /r/ never touches the roof of the mouth. If you can feel a bump, it is too far back.

✅ Say these R words

roll · run · rabbit · reach · ready · red · race

roll run rabbit reach

🎯 Teach it through the unit

Every time a child says roll or run, shape the /r/ with them: finger under the chin = tongue goes UP, no touch, lips forward.

I can roll. Let's run!

🗣️ Chant the R sounds

R-r-r — roll! R-r-r — run! R-r-r — rabbit! Short, loud, proud. Repeat 3 times, faster each round.

3× chant
