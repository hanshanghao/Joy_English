4 · Dialogue Game — What Comes Next?

15–20 MIN
🎯 Goal: from recognition → recall. Three levels, on screen.

Level 1 · Recall

"What comes next?" — choose the next line from three. No looking at the book.

Level 2 · Missing words

"How's the ______ up there?" — click the missing word.

Level 3 · Silent teacher

Teacher mimes, class reads the line. Big, loud, confident.

Scoring

⭐ Correct +1 · ⭐ Quick +1 · Wrong → another group steals it.
🔄 Transition: "New time — everybody stand up! We are going to MOVE!"

Teaching Notes

DIALOGUE

Comprehension first

Story retell with the poster before any drilling. Check with: "What's at the top?" → "A giant, angry crow!"

Then pronunciation

Stress: UP there · CHECK it out · BE careful.

Contractions

How is → How's · It is → It's · Don't = do not.

Feel the line

① curious → ② excited → ③ worried → ④ confident → ⑤ calling → ⑥ proud.
